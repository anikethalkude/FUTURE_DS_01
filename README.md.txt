# FUTURE_DS_01

## Business Sales Performance Analytics

### Objective
Analyze sales data to identify:
- Revenue trends
- Top selling products
- Country-wise revenue
- Customer insights

### Tools Used
- Tableau
- GitHub

### Dashboard KPIs
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Customers

### Visualizations
1. Revenue Trend
2. Top Products by Revenue
3. Country Revenue Analysis
4. Top Sold Products

### Key Insights
- United Kingdom generated the highest revenue.
- A few products contribute significantly to sales.
- Revenue varies across months.
